1. The Thesis
Residential real estate has been digitized horizontally.
Listings, payments, accounting, communication, and property management each have specialized software solutions.
However, the fundamental economic relationship remains fragmented.
Residents are treated as individual transactions. Agents are limited to conversion events. Owners and operators lack a unified intelligence layer connecting financial behavior, operational activity, and lifecycle value.
Reservatior is building the infrastructure layer that transforms residential real estate from a series of disconnected transactions into a continuously managed, high-trust financial relationship.

2. The Problem: The Missing Trust Lifecycle Layer
A modern residential lease depends on multiple disconnected systems:
	•	CRM platforms
	•	Listing systems
	•	Payment providers
	•	Maintenance software
	•	Accounting tools
	•	Communication channels
Each system optimizes one workflow.
Nobody owns the complete lifecycle:
Discovery → Qualification → Leasing → Payment → Maintenance → Renewal → Ownership Transition
The result:
	•	Operational friction
	•	Fragmented intelligence
	•	Duplicate workflows
	•	High vacancy costs
	•	Limited financial visibility
	•	Weak resident lifecycle retention

3. Our Wedge: Financial Onboarding & Loyalty Infrastructure
Reservatior begins with the highest-friction moment in residential leasing:
Upfront Financial Commitment
Traditional leasing requires large initial payments:
	•	First month rent
	•	Last month rent
	•	Security deposits
	•	Additional move-in costs
This creates unnecessary friction for qualified residents and reduces conversion opportunities.

Monthly Kumbara Deposit Model
Reservatior introduces a structured monthly financial model that distributes move-in obligations over time.
This creates:
Lower Friction
	•	Larger qualified applicant pools
	•	Faster leasing velocity
	•	Improved owner economics
Long-Term Relationship Formation
Every payment becomes a verified lifecycle event.
Residents build financial history.
Owners gain improved visibility.
The platform gains a continuously improving trust layer.
The initial lease becomes the foundation for a longer financial relationship.
