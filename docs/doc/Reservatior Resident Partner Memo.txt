Vision: A Better Way to Rent, Build Trust, and Move Forward

1. The Resident Problem
Renting a home has become unnecessarily difficult.
Qualified residents often face the same barriers:
	•	Large upfront move-in costs
	•	Complex application processes
	•	Limited transparency
	•	Repeating the same verification process for every move
	•	No financial benefit from being a reliable resident
Today, a resident can pay rent perfectly for years and still have to restart from zero every time they move.
The rental industry tracks transactions.
It does not build relationships.

2. Our Mission
Reservatior transforms renting from a series of disconnected payments into a continuous financial relationship.
We help residents:
	•	Move into homes with less friction
	•	Build a trusted rental profile
	•	Manage their housing lifecycle
	•	Create financial value from responsible behavior

3. The Wedge: Financially Smarter Move-In
Traditional renting requires significant upfront payments:
	•	First month rent
	•	Security deposits
	•	Additional move-in expenses
These barriers prevent many qualified residents from accessing the homes they want.
Reservatior introduces a structured monthly financial model.
Monthly Kumbara Model
Instead of concentrating financial obligations at move-in, residents can build their commitment gradually through predictable monthly contributions.
Benefits:
Easier Access
	•	Lower initial financial pressure
	•	More flexible move-in process
	•	Better matching between residents and available homes
Stronger Financial History
Every successful payment becomes part of a verified rental history.
Responsible residents build:
	•	Trust signals
	•	Reliability records
	•	Better future opportunities
